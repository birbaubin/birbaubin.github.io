## 🎓 **Teaching Philosophy**

I believe that **education is the foundation of innovation**. My teaching approach combines theoretical rigor with practical application, ensuring that students not only understand the concepts but can also apply them to real-world problems.

### **Core Principles**
- **🔬 Theory + Practice**: Balance fundamental concepts with hands-on implementation
- **🤝 Collaborative Learning**: Foster an environment where students learn from each other
- **💡 Problem-Solving Focus**: Emphasize critical thinking and creative solutions
- **🌍 Real-World Relevance**: Connect academic concepts to practical applications

## 📚 **Current Courses**

### **IFT-2001: Introduction to Computer Security**
*Fall 2024, Université du Québec à Montréal*

**Course Overview**: This course introduces students to fundamental concepts in computer security, from cryptographic primitives to system security.

**Topics Covered**:
- **🔐 Cryptography**: Symmetric and asymmetric encryption, hash functions
- **🛡️ Network Security**: Protocols, attacks, and defenses
- **🔒 System Security**: Operating system security, access control
- **🦠 Malware Analysis**: Types, detection, and prevention

**Teaching Approach**: 
- Interactive lectures with real-world examples
- Hands-on labs using security tools and frameworks
- Case studies from recent security incidents
- Group projects on security analysis

### **IFT-3001: Advanced Topics in Privacy-Preserving Technologies**
*Winter 2025, Université du Québec à Montréal*

**Course Overview**: Advanced course covering cutting-edge privacy-preserving technologies and their applications in machine learning and data analysis.

**Topics Covered**:
- **🔐 Homomorphic Encryption**: Theory and practical implementation
- **🤝 Secure Multiparty Computation**: Protocols and applications
- **🧠 Privacy-Preserving ML**: Federated learning and differential privacy
- **🔒 Zero-Knowledge Proofs**: Applications in blockchain and identity systems

**Teaching Approach**:
- Research paper discussions and presentations
- Implementation projects using real cryptographic libraries
- Guest lectures from industry experts
- Research-oriented final projects
